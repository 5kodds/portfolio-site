PolicyPilot

AI-assisted policy analysis designed to turn dense documents into clearer summaries, obligations, risks and actionable insights.

Open prototype ↗Discuss the product

THE PROBLEM

Important policy is often readable in theory and unusable in practice.

Policies, regulations and institutional documents contain decisions that matter, but the information people actually need is often buried inside long, technical language. The problem is not merely summarization. Different readers need to know different things: what changed, what applies to them, what creates risk and what action is required.

PolicyPilot is an exploration of how AI can become a practical interface between dense documents and human decision-making. A user can provide policy text and move from raw language toward clearer summaries, obligations, risks and follow-up questions.

The prototype is intentionally focused on comprehension rather than replacing expert judgment. Its purpose is to reduce the friction between reading a document and understanding what deserves attention.
