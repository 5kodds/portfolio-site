01 / ABOUT

I’m interested in the point where ideas stop being interesting and start becoming useful.

That has taken me across product development, operations, digital systems, entrepreneurship, hospitality, energy and agriculture. The industries change. The underlying question stays remarkably consistent: How do you turn a real problem into a system that works?

Today, much of my work is focused on artificial intelligence and the new capabilities it creates — but I am equally interested in what technology cannot decide for us: intent, judgment, responsibility and what is worth building in the first place.

I build from Africa with a global perspective.
