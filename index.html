01 ABOUT

THE THROUGH-LINE

I’m interested in the point where ideas stop being interesting and start becoming useful.

My work has moved through product development, operations, digital systems, entrepreneurship, hospitality, energy and agriculture. The industries change. The underlying question stays remarkably consistent: How do you turn a real problem into a system that works?

Today, much of my attention is on artificial intelligence and the capabilities it unlocks, while remaining equally interested in what technology cannot decide for us: intent, judgment, responsibility and what is worth building in the first place.

I build from Africa with a global perspective.
