How fulfilment works

A few useful details.

What happens when I gift a digital copy?

After verified payment and when fulfilment opens, each recipient receives an email containing a secure download/access link. Your optional personal message is included in the recipient email.

What happens when I gift a paperback?

The recipient receives a secure claim link by email and uses it to provide or confirm delivery details. This lets you give a physical book without needing the recipient's postal address at checkout.

Can I gift more than one copy?

Yes. Multiple gifting is built into the checkout. Each copy has its own recipient and fulfilment entitlement.

How does sponsoring readers work?

You choose the exact number of readers. Each sponsored reader corresponds to one entitlement. Allocation and delivery will be tracked after payment rather than assumed at checkout.

Why can't I pay yet?

This is the live checkout preview while Lemon Squeezy and NOWPayments verification is completed and the remaining payment rails are configured. No card, bank or crypto details are collected on this page yet.
