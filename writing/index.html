01 CURRENT THEMES

AI + HUMAN AGENCY

What AI changes — and what it doesn’t.

AI changes the economics of execution. Tasks that once required significant time, specialist skill or organizational capacity can increasingly be compressed into a conversation with a capable system. That matters. But faster execution does not answer the upstream questions: what should be done, which constraints matter, what evidence deserves trust, and who is responsible for the consequences.

The interesting future is therefore not “human versus machine.” It is the design of a working relationship in which machine capability expands what people can accomplish while human judgment remains responsible for direction.

AFRICA + INFRASTRUCTURE

Why the next chapter will be built.

Many African markets are described through what they lack: reliable energy, logistics, financing, formal data, efficient distribution or dependable public infrastructure. Those gaps are real. They are also where unusually valuable businesses can emerge.

The opportunity is often not another consumer app. It is a missing operating layer: software that coordinates fragmented suppliers, financing that makes infrastructure affordable, energy systems that turn unreliable grids into predictable service, or better operations that convert informal activity into dependable commerce.

BUSINESS + SYSTEMS

Ideas become useful when they become systems.

An idea is not yet a business, a product or even a reliable solution. It becomes useful when the important parts can repeat: customers can understand it, delivery can happen consistently, feedback changes the system, money can move through it, and quality does not depend on one heroic burst of effort.

This is why I am increasingly interested in systems rather than ideas alone. The most valuable work is often the translation layer between insight and repeatability.
